### The between-reports problem: why security teams miss what attackers see
- **Tags:** `#web` `#api` `#cloud`
- **Severity:** info · **Hunt:** 1/5 · **Score:** 1.0 · **Status:** unknown · **Age:** 0d
- **Sources:** [1](https://www.intigriti.com/blog/business-insights/why-security-teams-miss-what-attackers-see)

This article highlights the visibility gap between automated discovery reports and actual attacker intent, noting that security teams often miss critical context between reports. It argues that while scanners are necessary, teams need earlier signals to understand attacker focus before the next vulnerability lands. Relevant for bug bounty hunters to prioritize targets based on attacker intent rather than just report volume.
