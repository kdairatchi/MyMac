# 14-Day Hunt Sprint — 2026-05-14 → 2026-05-28

> **North star:** Submit at least **2 valid findings** in 14 days, total bounty target **$2,000+** by 2026-06-14.
> May closed: **$890** (Hostinger SSRF). 2x = $1,800 · 3x = $2,700 · 4x = $3,560.

## Daily ritual (every day, 5 hours total)

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 10 min | Read previous SESSION.md for active target. Resume from last checkpoint. |
| Morning | 10 min | `payout-analyst` quick check. Confirm class focus matches ROI. |
| Morning | 10 min | Intel refresh: `daily-cve-intel` + `technique-scout` for any new patterns relevant to active targets. |
| Hunt | 4 hours | One target × one OWASP class. Caido capture everything. |
| Evening | 30 min | Write session log. Park leads. Plan tomorrow. |

## Target rotation rules
- **One PRIMARY target per day** — no parallel hunts
- **Two leads max per session** — anything beyond goes to LEAD column
- **Pivot trigger:** 3+ probe-only logs without movement = move on
- **Park trigger:** ceiling clearly Low → park in SESSION.md, hunt different class on different target

## Validation gates
- Every claimed finding → `validator` agent (7-Question Gate)
- Every report → `report-writer` only after validator passes
- Every finding (won or lost) → `loss-curator` writes the lesson into memory
